Why it matters

Your website is doing more work than you think

For most service businesses, a website isn't a brochure. It's often the first, and only, impression a time-pressured visitor gets before deciding whether to get in touch. Confusing navigation or a buried contact form doesn't just look dated: it costs enquiries you never hear about, because the person just leaves.

A short review is usually enough to find where that's happening on your site, whatever you do.

Research for the Swansea Women's Aid project surfaced a recurring pattern: visitors often weren't sure whether what they were experiencing counted as abuse, or what support was actually available. That gap reshaped the entire homepage around getting people to the right information faster. Insight from the Swansea Women's Aid research phase
